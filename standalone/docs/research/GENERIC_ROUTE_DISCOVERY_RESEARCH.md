## 1. Recommendation

**Proposal:** retain a coverage-preserving geometric decomposition or equivalent roadmap as the generic spatial substrate, but replace “one independent search for every ordered overlapping-box walk” with a **lazy, contact-aware interaction graph/automaton**. Use that representation to discover potential support and obstacle-passage choices and to derive progress guides. Refine it with scoped movement/reachability information; leave physical feasibility to existing movement construction, solving, and replay. This combines the connectivity role of exact geometric planning, the separation of contact modes in multimodal planning, and feedback-guided layering exemplified by SyCLoP. It does not inherit any of their completeness theorems automatically. [Exact cell decomposition][L1], [contact-mode planning][L6], [SyCLoP][L7]; current integration points: [`regions.build_route_graph`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`routes.enumerate_semantic_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [`checkpoint_search.evaluate_program`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/engine.py).

**Proposal:** a route should retain potential **contact events, approach/escape interactions, meaningful 3D passages, ordered revisits, and their unresolved alternatives**, rather than just landing blocks or every raw cell identifier. Which approach distinctions remain active is conditional on the movement family and its conservative swept envelope. An unwanted interaction proved absent can disappear; a required contact proved absent rejects its proposal. Those two consequences must stay different. This follows the existing shared eligibility contract, including its full-proposal and ownership requirements. [`InteractionGate.query/required`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`normal_choices`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/normal.py), [governing relevance invariant](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

**Proposal:** represent the potentially cyclic language first and generate particular walks lazily. A finite graph can represent repeated contact/passages without eagerly listing every resulting itinerary. Avoid a universal simple-path/no-revisit rule: the program carries momentum, time and contact history, so a revisit can affect its continuations. Finite execution budgets suspend exploration; they do not turn the represented language into a certificate that all alternatives were explored. [Reachability graphs and systematic exploration][L4], [`motion_record` and `solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`route_queue.search_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/corridor_queue.py).

The user's B2→B3 example is sound with a precise qualification: remove **premature side/corner avoidance** only where it is proved irrelevant over the entire declared family and collision phase. Keep the required B3 landing footprint/contact. If left/right descriptions subsequently express the same unrestricted flight family, merge them before expensive products. If either still expresses a real restriction, preserve its disjunction or retain an explicitly optimistic guide which must be refined before it can certify feasibility. The formal conditions are in §7. [`block_interaction`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`ConstraintVariants.effective_key/option`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [`landing_wall_options`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/cases.py), [full-proposal/ownership contract](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

## 2. What “all potential routes” should mean

### 2.1 Representing potential behavior is different from enumerating trajectories

**Proposed contract:** fix a finite modeled scene, player/body model, initial state or initial-state family, supported interaction repertoire, task predicate, and declared movement horizon when one is imposed. Let `T` be the set of physically valid executions within that contract. A spatial abstraction `A` has the desired coverage if every execution in `T` has at least one representation in `A`. The abstraction may also admit spurious candidates; cheap necessary checks and later decisions/solves remove those. This is an overapproximation target, not a requirement to list all individual continuous trajectories. The distinction mirrors connectivity-preserving roadmaps and conservative abstraction/refinement, while the concrete execution contract already exists in the repository's solve scope and identity. [Roadmap accessibility/connectivity][L2], [abstraction/refinement][L12], [`solve_scope/solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py).

A geometric planner's usual completeness claim concerns finding some collision-free connection when one exists. That alone does not assert enumeration of every homotopy class, every contact sequence, or every dynamically realizable route. Dynamics add velocity and action history; contact constraints add mode-dependent, often lower-dimensional sets. [Exact geometric roadmap conditions][L2], [planning under dynamics][L3], [multimodal constraints][L6].

**Derivation:** a graph with a cycle can compactly represent arbitrarily many walks, for example `B1 → B2 → B1 → B2 → … → goal`. An execution horizon bounds which realizations are eligible, but a materialized route-count cap merely limits how many have been serviced. Therefore report three quantities separately: what the abstraction represents, what has been generated, and what has been physically checked. The current queue already distinguishes a bounded catalogue, pending spatial generation and independent inner continuations; it must not report those as exhaustive equivalence classes. [Reachability graphs][L4], [`enumerate_semantic_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [`search_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/corridor_queue.py).

### 2.2 Proposed hierarchy of equivalence

The following is a **design definition**, motivated by geometric topology, contact-mode planning and the current exact-context model keys. It deliberately separates grouping for guidance from stronger equivalence needed to discard work. [Topology distinctions][L8], [multimodal planning][L6], [`program_physics_key`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/program.py), [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py).

| Level | Proposed meaning | What it permits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geometric location | Player-reference positions in free space, with explicit boundary/contact strata | Connectivity and candidate passage discovery; no statement that a jump can realize them |
| Contact/passage family | Ordered required support/contact events and meaningful passage alternatives, including allowed revisits | Shared route-specific progress interpretation; multiple timings and movement programs can belong here |
| Scoped interaction refinement | A family plus a declared input/contact/phase context and its active approach/avoidance alternatives | Conservative irrelevance filtering and meaningful approach branching |
| Fixed physical model | Exact physics, original state, scope, constraints, contact decisions and relevant resource/ownership state | Exact effective-model dedup where contexts and continuation consequences agree |
| Validated execution | A particular continuous solution passing the declared physics, scene and route/task checks | Feasibility evidence for that execution, not for every program in its family |

Do not equate the levels. Identical landing lists can hide different corner approaches; identical box walks can contain different contacts; identical numerical endpoints do not establish identical histories. The source already stores separate physical-program, solve-scope and route identities, and the contact-aware progress audit explicitly distinguishes support arrival from free-space occupancy. [`solve_spec.py`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`route_progress.advance/_contact`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/progress.py), [`SemanticCorridorAdapter.program_states/candidate`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/semantic_adapter.py).

### 2.3 A route family should be cyclic and partially specified

**Proposal:** the reusable outer object is an event graph or automaton with support/mode nodes, spatial passage alternatives and conservative unknown links. A selected family may specify a prefix and leave later branches unresolved. Producing one guide does not require eagerly fixing every future obstacle-side decision or every input tick. Meaningful contacts and passage revisits remain ordered; local setup motions can be silent at the outer abstraction while their actual inputs, momentum, constraints and resource use remain in the movement program. [Layered guidance and feedback][L7], [`movement_frontier.persistent_programs`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/frontier.py), [`depth_first.search_goals`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/dfs.py), [`motion_record`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py).

“Silent” here means omitted from the **outer route label**, not deleted from physics or treated as a zero-input move. No new same-endpoint dominance rule or sibling-decision transfer follows from sharing an outer family. This preserves the imported original-state solve and single-owner continuation contracts. [`movement_composition.append_choice/compile_program`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/program.py), [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [DFS implementation contract](../design/DFS_INTERACTION_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

## 3. What the literature supports, and what it does not

### 3.1 Comparison of candidate foundations

This table is an evidence-backed comparison followed by a project-specific suitability judgment. Each guarantee belongs to its cited method and assumptions, not to the current code merely because the terminology is similar.

| Method | Useful property and qualification | Suitability judgment for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact geometric cell decomposition | A finite cell structure with easy local connections, query attachment and correct adjacency reduces geometric planning to graph search. Ordinary geometric results do not establish contact/dynamics coverage. [L1][L1], [L2][L2] | **Preferred baseline substrate** for finite collision-box maps, provided boundary contacts and coverage are explicit. Avoid promoting incidental subdivision walks to semantic routes. |
| Adaptive cells / lazy refinement | Resolution-based guarantees depend on systematic refinement and search; the cited control example assumes a particular simple dynamics model. An incomplete cover needs a separate connectivity-preservation obligation. [L4][L4], [approximate-cover discussion][L5] | **Preferred scaling direction**, with unresolved cells retained optimistically and exact obstacle-aligned refinements where needed. This is a proposal, not an existing adaptive implementation. |
| Direct visibility / clearance roadmaps | Complete roadmaps can be built without a cell decomposition if accessibility and connectivity are preserved. A complete method for one geometric problem class is not automatically a complete 3D contact planner. [L2][L2] | A legitimate alternative backend. A graph of handpicked block centers/corners requires an additional coverage argument. |
| Overlapping convex regions, including IRIS-style inflation | Large convex safe regions can simplify local optimization; seeded inflation alone need not cover all free space. The IRIS paper explicitly discusses trading coverage for fewer regions. [L9][L9] | Useful local domains or shortcuts over a retained covering substrate. Do not use arbitrary overlap walks as the sole route-equivalence definition. Our current cover is a separate algorithm, not IRIS. |
| Graphs of convex sets (GCS) | Optimizes continuous positions in supplied convex vertex sets together with path selection using a mixed-integer convex formulation. Guarantees concern that supplied graph/formulation. [L10][L10] | Potential guide-geometry optimizer later. It does not construct a complete parkour route catalogue or make the yaw/contact problem convex. Not recommended as this refactor's first change. |
| Homotopy / homology signatures | Topological invariants can distinguish some obstacle-passage classes and augment graph search. Homology equality is not general homotopy equality; both concern specified spaces. [L8][L8], [L13][L13] | Useful supplementary signatures and duplicate diagnostics. **Insufficient alone** for landing-vs-overflight or phase/height-conditioned corner approaches (§4). |
| Sampling roadmaps / trees | Probabilistic or resolution guarantees require their sampling/local-planning assumptions and limiting exploration. Constraint manifolds need suitable representations; finite runs have no blanket all-routes guarantee. [L4][L4], [L11][L11] | Optional fallback/refinement machinery, especially for difficult reachable-state regions. Not a reason to replace existing analytical contact builders with uniform position sampling. |
| Multimodal contact planning | Models feasible sets by contact modes and explicitly sampled/constructed mode transitions. Its convergence results assume suitable mode and transition coverage/expansiveness. [L6][L6] | **Best conceptual model for route semantics**, combined with the geometric substrate and existing movement builders. |
| SyCLoP-style layering | Uses workspace decomposition to produce high-level leads; low-level dynamic exploration feeds information back to subsequent leads. [L7][L7] | **Closest architectural precedent** for the requested potential-route guidance. Our DFS/local NLP is not SyCLoP's low-level planner, so its results cannot be imported. |
| Multilevel / quotient-space planning | Coarse paths can guide higher-dimensional planning; formal results rely on admissible abstractions and the paper's sampling assumptions. [L14][L14] | Supports the coarse-geometry → contact/phase → fixed-model hierarchy. Rejecting a relaxed necessary condition can be useful; passing it is not a physical feasibility proof. |
| Lazy checking / abstraction refinement | Lazy PRM validates candidate roadmap pieces on demand. CEGAR refines an overapproximation when an abstract witness is demonstrated spurious. [L15][L15], [L12][L12] | Supports deferred interaction expansion and scoped proof-driven refinement. An NLP timeout is not a demonstrated spurious route and must not become a permanent no-good. |

**Recommendation, inferred from this comparison:** use exact/coverage-preserving geometry plus a lazy hybrid contact/interaction abstraction. Cells, roadmap edges, convex regions and topological signatures are complementary tools. No cited result singles out one data structure as universally the most robust method for all geometry, dynamics and contacts. The current project can reuse its box geometry and movement builders rather than replace them wholesale with a new sampling or mixed-integer solver. [Geometric alternatives][L2], [dynamics][L3], [contact modes][L6]; [`geometry.decompose`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/cells.py), [`InteractionGate`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py).

### 3.2 Contact sets and narrow passages require separate treatment

A support surface is not just a positive-volume free-space region: exact contact introduces equality constraints. Sampling uniformly in an ambient configuration space need not hit a lower-dimensional constraint set. Constraint-aware state spaces and mode-transition constructions exist precisely to address that issue. This does not mean forward simulation with collision events has zero probability of landing; it means a generic ambient-position sampling guarantee cannot simply be applied to every contact boundary. [OMPL constrained-planning documentation][L11], [Hauser–Latombe mode/transition framework][L6].

**Project consequence:** retain the existing analytic descent/contact proposal path (`first_descent`, finite top-contact boxes and landing rows), exact exposed support unions, and shared physical validation. Do not approximate away zero-height support regions by insisting every routing state have positive 3D volume. Conversely, a line/point remnant in geometric coverage is not automatically a valid finite landing area; physical overlap and the selected contact builder still decide it. In particular, `build_route_graph` does not create a support incidence from a patch touching only the outer rim of the owner's expanded X/Z footprint: it checks strict overlap with those bounds. Boundary and numerical-clearance conventions must match the shared contact predicates, rather than introduce all geometric strata as usable support arrivals. [`normal_choices/first_descent`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/normal.py), [`support_components/canonical_cover/build_route_graph`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`physical_events`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/route_guards.py).

## 4. Route identity needs both contact-aware passage structure and constraint semantics

### 4.1 Why ordinary 3D homotopy is insufficient

The original 3D topological-planning overview explicitly illustrates that an isolated solid cube in unrestricted 3D space does not induce multiple path homotopy classes. It also distinguishes homology from homotopy despite the paper's title. Thus “left of this finite block” and “right of it” need not be globally different homotopy classes: a deformation may pass over its top. [Bhattacharya–Likhachev–Kumar, pp.1–2 and Fig.3][L8].

**Derivation for parkour:** that deformation may be unavailable within the allowed jump-height envelope, timing, incoming momentum or mandatory target contact. Two approaches that are geometrically deformable in unrestricted XYZ can therefore remain physically distinct choices in a particular movement family. Conversely, two face labels may become irrelevant once the entire applicable sweep is proved clear. We need **contact-aware, mode/phase-relative passage distinctions**, not just a position-space homotopy label or a fixed list of global X/Z side tests. [Dynamics and state-space dependence][L3]; [`reachable_envelopes`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py), [`landing_wall_options/corner_rows`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/cases.py), [`InteractionGate.query/required`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py).

The more general homotopy work studies fundamental-group presentations and word problems for specified classes of configuration spaces, underscoring that a topology signature requires assumptions about the space being classified. It supplies no theorem identifying our contact-aware route families with simple per-obstacle left/right labels. [Bhattacharya–Ghrist, abstract and construction scope][L13].

### 4.2 Two complementary notions

**Proposal:** use a spatial/contact signature to organize **potential** choices and use scoped language/constraint equivalence to remove **redundant** descriptions. Neither replaces the other. A topological distinction suggests which alternative to retain; exact effective constraints and continuation state determine when two instantiated descriptions can safely share or discard numerical work. The current code already separates semantic route keys, physical-program keys and solve identities, but its passage key still includes changes of transit-box identity. [`semantic_events`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [`program_physics_key/program_search_key`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/program.py), [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`ConstraintVariants.effective_key`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py).

**Proposed spatial/contact signature:** ordered support/mode transitions; finite passage components associated with actual obstacle features; approach/escape side or corner classes where relevant; and explicit revisit occurrences. Include over/under and contact-vs-overflight distinctions. Leave exact input timings and headings in the lower-level program unless a declared phase context is needed for a refinement. The signature may be partial and branch lazily. This is a design synthesis of contact modes and the repository's existing interaction/context machinery, not a claimed complete topological invariant. [Mode structure][L6], [`RouteTransition`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`route_progress.advance`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/progress.py), [`InteractionGate`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py).

### 4.3 Avoid arbitrary subdivision identity

Current `semantic_events` appends an `open_portal` passage when the source/target transit keys differ; the keys are derived from transit-region geometry. Therefore two walks through overlapping cuboids can remain distinct even if they share substantial physical movement. This is an implementation fact, not proof that those walks should all be merged. [`geometry_key/free_transit_cover/build_route_graph`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`semantic_events`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py).

**Proposal:** contract or summarize incidental subdivisions only when the summary retains the relevant entering/leaving portal relation, contact events and continuation alternatives. A macroedge must represent the allowed internal language/relation, not just the union of all its boxes. Combining opposite passages into one volume union can admit path splicing that none of the original variants allowed; the existing per-variant progress cursor is an important protection to preserve. [`initial_cursor/advance`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/progress.py), [`SemanticCorridorAdapter.for_goal`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/semantic_adapter.py), [spatial-refactor language contract](../design/SPATIAL_CORRIDOR_REFACTOR_PLAN.md).

## 5. What a discovered route should contain

The following is a **proposed representation contract**, extending concepts already present in `RouteRegion`, `RouteTransition`, `Corridor`, solve scopes and owned physical programs. It is not a request to rename those APIs before measuring the representation. [`regions.py`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`Corridor`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [`solve_spec.py`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [owned-row relevance contract](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

| Record | Include | Do not substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain/context | Geometry/body revision, initial-state family, supported mechanics, horizon if imposed, contact/phase assumptions, modeled scope | A fixture name or one optimized trajectory |
| Required contact/mode event | Physical owner, exposed component/patch set, contact type, approach/outgoing relation, occurrence identity | A point near a block or raw height equality alone |
| Potential interaction | Obstacle feature/component, affected swept phase/window, role (`required`, `unwanted`, `approach prerequisite`), capability status, refinement alternatives | A global “avoid B3” flag |
| Passage alternative | Connected opening or obstacle-side/layer relation, allowable entry/exit sets, constraints/guards when committed, provenance | An unqualified cell-ID word or single representative portal midpoint |
| Abstract continuation | Cyclic/event graph or automaton state, latent alternative language, branch provenance and deferred refinement | An eagerly exhausted list of paths |
| Instantiated continuation | Exact movement program, owner completion states, resource/cap histories, required future events, accepted/unknown fixed models | Endpoint position/velocity alone |
| Progress guide | Ordered guide regions/anchors, stage achievement predicates, outgoing directions and correspondence to the route context | A hard equality forcing every anchor point to be visited |
| Evidence | Scoped exclusion certificates, unresolved reasons, exact request identities and replay tiers | A timeout interpreted as no route |

An illustrative outer fragment could be written as follows. This is **design pseudocode**; actual branch existence and relevance remain queries to geometry and the shared movement context. [`InteractionGate`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`landing_wall_options`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/cases.py), [`ConstraintVariants`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py).

```text
support(B2)
  → potential approach to B3
      { ordinary free approach
      | left-corner approach
      | right-corner approach
      | supported named interaction }
  → required top contact on B3
  → outgoing flight/passage alternatives
```

**Proposal:** do not create four independent solved routes merely because the grammar can name four branches. Query relevance, preserve active branches symbolically, normalize vacuous branches and only materialize distinctions that change the allowed passage/contact language or useful guidance. If an approach remains unknown, it can stay a latent refinement. This reuses the existing principle “eligibility before alternatives/products,” rather than constructing every named description then removing rows after a solve. [`InteractionGate.context/query`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`ConstraintVariants.option/effective_key`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [generation relevance §§1–3](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

**Proposal:** guide-specific progress should organize work within a consistent route/stage interpretation; time to one route's B2 is not a dominance comparison against time to another route's different support. Guides can be shared without proving that the underlying executions have interchangeable futures. All completed routes still use the common task objective—on current Real11, minimum achieved height7 finish tick followed by maximum Z at that tick. This preserves the existing common-goal comparison and terminal-ranking contracts instead of introducing a new weighted score. [Common-goal and current terminal objective](../design/DFS_INTERACTION_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md), [`comparison_goals.py`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/goals.py), [`candidate_rank`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/ranking.py).

## 6. Worked B2→B3 cases

### 6.1 Numerical vertical illustration: a higher target has a vulnerable approach phase

This is a **reproduced hypothetical ordinary profile**, not proof of any complete Real11 B2→B3 trajectory. Starting at feet height6 with ordinary rest carry and jumping immediately, the shared scalar replay gives heights approximately `6.000, 6.420, 6.753, 7.001, 7.166, 7.249, 7.252, 7.177, 7.024, 6.797`. `first_descent(...,7)` returns input8. Thus the player's feet start below a height7 target top, clear it, and later descend through its height. The helper and input context are fully specified below. [`replay/first_descent`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/normal.py), [`REST_CARRY`](../../src/parkour_solver/physics/vertical.py).

```sh
python3 -B - <<'PY'
from parkour_solver.movement.normal import replay, first_descent
from parkour_solver.physics.vertical import REST_CARRY
trace = replay(dict(position=6., velocity=REST_CARRY, onGround=True),
               12, jumping=True, threshold=0.)
print(trace['position'][:10])
print(first_descent(trace, 7.))
PY
```

**Derivation:** horizontal distance to the target can affect whether its vertical side is encountered while feet are still low. Therefore the landing target itself can be a relevant avoidance/approach obstacle before becoming the intended support. This must be checked on complete Y→X→Z swept phases, not inferred from one height sample or the final landing. Named step/grind probes require the existing broader unknown bounds; this ordinary illustration cannot exclude those other mechanics. [`reachable_envelopes`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py), [`normal_choices`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/normal.py), [`block_interaction`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py).

### 6.2 Far enough that early side avoidance is absent

**Proposed decision sequence:** construct an optimistic envelope for the declared B2→B3 movement family and its relevant pre-landing phases. If B3's expanded side-interaction region is unreachable throughout that window, omit its premature left/right/corner avoidance branches. The later B3 top-contact guard and finite landing footprint remain required. “B3 is too far to matter now” and “B3 can never be landed on” are different claims, with different scopes. [`InteractionGate.query/required`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`block_interaction`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`normal_choices`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/normal.py), [required-vs-unwanted rule](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

If those descriptions were generated solely to encode avoidance of that now-absent interaction, omit the whole avoidance choice and retain ordinary flight in the declared window. This does **not** require every arbitrary left/right inequality to be individually implied: a distant obstacle can be harmless while “stay left of its X coordinate” still unnecessarily restricts flight. The normalization should preserve the intended physical avoidance semantics; an overrestrictive old face grammar is not the coverage oracle. Keep the later required landing, other-obstacle conditions, input histories and resource alternatives. If B3 is unreachable even at its required contact, reject the entire proposed landing program instead. Do not make an impossible required landing “feasible” by deleting its rows. [Formal physical-vs-encoded distinction in §7](#7-safe-pruning-and-merging-rules), [`InteractionGate.required`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [generation relevance §§1,4](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

### 6.3 Near enough that a side or corner can matter

**Proposed decision sequence:** when the conservative sweep overlaps B3 while feet/body placement can collide with a side before the top contact, retain the candidate approach distinctions. Build them from actual relevant faces/corners and their phase windows, not a permanent dominant-axis sign attached to the whole block. Two candidates with the same eventual B3 contact can then have different approach constraints and different attainable arrival/momentum outcomes. The source already supports face chains, corner rows, finite side contact and approach prerequisites; it should supply these choices rather than a second Python collision model. [`ConstraintVariants`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [`corner_rows/landing_wall_options`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/cases.py), [`open_stepping_choices`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/stepping.py), [`reachable_envelopes`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py).

An overlap test says only that the interaction has not been ruled out. It does not prove left or right is physically feasible, and a failed local NLP for one fixed model does not reject all approach timings or contact methods. Refinement should retain those unknown alternatives and their exact contexts. [`interaction_reachability`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py), [`solve_contact_branches`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/repair.py), [solver/request and uncertainty contract](../design/DFS_INTERACTION_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

### 6.4 One active side or an active disjunction

**Derivation:** if only the left predicate becomes vacuous, the right branch need not become identical to it; one may contain the other or consume different continuation resources. If left/right jointly express the remaining physical free-space condition, their union may be represented compactly, but the union still carries that condition. Dropping an active `(left OR right)` constraint can admit trajectories through the obstacle. Only a proved full-family equivalence allows replacing the union by unrestricted flight. [Formal language rules in §7](#7-safe-pruning-and-merging-rules), [`ConstraintVariants.option`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [`blockage_counts`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/avoidance.py).

### 6.5 Changing the context can restore the distinction

An exclusion for a fixed jump/profile/horizon or a modeled prefix cannot be applied to an untimed root route with different future contacts or velocities. In the actual implementation, `InteractionGate.query` intentionally sets approach Y to unknown before an interaction outcome is chosen; `required(program)` then checks the proposed full events. This prevents reasoning from a realized feet-height trace from silently removing a valid named step/ceiling possibility. Context changes must invalidate or weaken the proof, rather than reuse the result by block ID alone. [`InteractionGate.query/required`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`reachable_envelopes`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py), [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py).

## 7. Safe pruning and merging rules

### 7.1 Definitions and direction of inference

The following is a **formal design derivation**, not an external theorem about the current implementation. It makes precise the existing relevance, dedup and ownership contracts. Let `C` include original state/family, body and geometry, supported mechanics, fixed/proposed inputs and phase assumptions, selected contacts, scope, remaining goals, resource limits and commitments. Let `F_C` be dynamically admissible candidate traces **together with their continuation-relevant state/resource/ownership histories** under the already declared constraints of `C`, before imposing the optional interaction/avoidance condition currently being considered. Some candidates may still violate that optional obstacle condition; this prevents defining “unwanted collision is absent” tautologically by assuming final collision validity in advance. Let `I` mean that physical interaction occurs in the declared swept phase/window. Final acceptance must still check every modeled obstacle and required event. [`solve_scope/solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`program_physics_key`](../../src/parkour_solver/movement/program.py), [relevance and ownership invariant](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

Suppose a checked outer bound `E_C` contains every relevant trace/sweep in `F_C`. Then `E_C ∩ I = ∅` implies `F_C ∩ I = ∅`: a subset of a disjoint set is disjoint. This is the sound direction used to remove obviously impossible choices. `E_C ∩ I ≠ ∅` proves no existence result because the bound may contain spurious states. The repository's interval/optimistic travel tests implement this necessary-condition style; numerical tolerances and optional LP refinement are not rational global certificates. [`reachable_envelopes/interaction_reachability`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py), [`annotate_normal_proposals`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`linear_reachability.py`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/linear.py).

### 7.2 Required interaction versus unwanted interaction

For an **unwanted** interaction, avoidance is `not I`. If `F_C ∩ I = ∅`, every trace in `F_C` already avoids it. Its avoidance predicate is vacuous in this scope, so no obstacle-side decision or interaction-derived guard is needed there. For a **required** contact/precondition, the proposed family is `F_C ∩ I`; the same absence proof makes that family empty and rejects the proposal. These are elementary set consequences, and coincide with the documented shared interaction contract. [`InteractionGate.required`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`ConstraintVariants.__init__`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [generation relevance §1](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

The **physical predicate** must be distinguished from one particular constraint grammar used to encode it. Suppose a block is far north of all admissible traces while those traces span both sides of its X coordinate. Physical avoidance is then automatic, but a generated “stay left” inequality and a generated “stay right” inequality can each be restrictive. The correct early operation is to detect the absent interaction and generate neither unnecessary branch. It is not to require those arbitrary rows to be tautologies before recognizing the block's irrelevance. If the old grammar omitted a valid “remain south” family, restoring it is a correction of overrestriction, not an unsound expansion of intended physical semantics. Independently required task/passage commitments still remain. This is a **derived example** of the physical `not I` semantics above and the documented eligibility-before-enumeration rule. [`ConstraintVariants.__init__/effective_key`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [generation relevance §§1–2](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

Absence must cover the queried Y/X/Z phase windows, including intended/unclipped probes where the supported mechanic needs them. A sample endpoint outside a block, or an optimized path that misses it, is not a bound over `F_C`. Unknown geometry/probe coverage retains a choice; an interaction removed from one context may become relevant after the program changes. [`reachable_envelopes`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/intervals.py), [`block_interaction`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [named probe uncertainty](../CURRENT_STATE.md).

### 7.3 Exact description merging

Let `L_C(R)` be the admitted trace/continuation language of description `R` in context `C`. Replacing descriptions `R1…Rk` by a merged representation `M` is exact when:

```text
L_C(M) = L_C(R1) ∪ … ∪ L_C(Rk)
```

This is a **definition of exact merging of correctly interpreted route alternatives**. It is a separate operation from repairing an overrestrictive encoding of a physically irrelevant interaction (§7.2). Equal effective rows are a useful sufficient component for fixed-model dedup only when the other context, remaining items and continuation resource/commitment state also agree. The current effective-row key deliberately removes rows already guaranteed by the envelope; existing policy also preserves distinctions relevant to caps and ownership. [`ConstraintVariants.effective_key/option`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [generation relevance §§2–3](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

Replacing the alternatives by unrestricted flight specifically requires `L_C(M) = F_C`. It is enough that their predicates are individually vacuous, or that their union provably covers all of `F_C` with the same continuation consequences. Mere equality of endpoints, guide points, or the currently best solved trajectory does not establish that equality. This follows directly from the definition above and the fact that resource/owner histories are part of `F_C`. [`motion_record`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`blockage_usage/within_caps`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/avoidance.py), [ownership/dedup requirements](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

If two equal row sets consume different face-chain/blockage resources and those resources change remaining choices, either retain both states or represent their alternative resource outcomes explicitly. Do not infer continuation equivalence from the numerical rows alone. A valid geometric shortcut must not silently bypass configured physical blockage caps. [`blockage_counts/blockage_usage`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/avoidance.py), [`ConstraintVariants`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [imported spatial integration requirements](../design/SPATIAL_CORRIDOR_REFACTOR_PLAN.md).

### 7.4 Optimistic guide grouping is a weaker operation

**Proposal:** the outer planner may deliberately use a broader object `G` with `union_i L_C(Ri) ⊆ L_C(G)` as an optimistic guide. That can simplify progress planning while preserving potential coverage, provided its latent lower-level alternatives remain recoverable. Mark it **unresolved guidance**, not an equivalent fixed model or a feasibility certificate. Physical checks must still enforce the actual selected decisions and refine/validate the eventual contact/passage behavior. This is an application of overapproximation/refinement, not an exact merge. [Conservative abstractions][L12], [`solve_scope`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`SemanticCorridorAdapter.candidate`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/semantic_adapter.py).

Sharing a guide does not require merging actual decision programs, copying chosen children between siblings, or erasing distinct solved/unknown requests. Exact caches can share construction/results only under their verified keys, and a result used under another route still needs that route's acceptance checks. Decision transfer remains outside this recommendation's implementation scope. [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`search_routes` immutable work owners](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/corridor_queue.py), [DFS ownership and deferred transfer](../design/DFS_INTERACTION_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

### 7.5 Required proof scope and lifecycle

**Proposed proof record:** geometry/body revision; original state/family; mechanics/input domain; source program/family; interaction owner and role; phase/window; envelope/constraint hash; modeled blocks and horizon; conclusion (`absent`, `required impossible`, `entailed`, `equivalent`); numerical tolerance/uncertainty; and the contexts in which it remains valid. Refinement/projection must retain the proof's owner or invalidate its use. This records the dependencies already used by solve scopes, model keys and owned constraints. [`solve_identity/solve_scope`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`InteractionGate.envelopes`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [full-proposal/projection invariant](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

**Proposed status lattice:** `not_ruled_out`, `excluded_in_C`, `validated_execution_in_C`, and `unknown_due_to_budget_or_missing_model`. Do not promote the last two to universal feasibility or impossibility. A global route exclusion requires a proof over all its admissible realizations; a failed fixed request only changes its own pending work/status. [`solve_contact_branches`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/repair.py), [`route_queue.search_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/corridor_queue.py), [results interpretation](../RESULTS_AND_DEBUGGING.md).

## 8. Geometry backend and graph construction recommendation

### 8.1 Preserve a geometric coverage contract

**Proposal:** start with the existing exact collision-box subtraction, player expansion, exposed support extraction and incidence primitives. Specify a finite query domain derived from modeled scene/reach/horizon assumptions; preserve the residual free space and relevant contact strata. Build explicit connected portal components. Local adaptive refinement is acceptable only if unrefined residuals remain represented and the claimed guarantee matches the refinement policy. This is the practical use of cell/roadmap connectivity requirements, not a proposal to run high-dimensional cylindrical algebraic decomposition over the entire physics state. [Geometric cell and roadmap requirements][L1], [L2][L2]; [`decompose/cell_portals/contact_patches`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/cells.py), [`support_components`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`collision_request`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/corridor_queue.py).

For an axis-aligned finite model, a **possible exact reference construction** is an arrangement of obstacle/query coordinate planes with free boxes and explicit boundary strata, followed by safe merging. The number of Cartesian boxes is the product of interval counts across the three axes; this elementary construction can become large, so it is a useful oracle for small cases rather than an automatic scalability recommendation. Existing subtraction/local refinement is another construction to test against that reference. This is a project-specific geometric construction proposal based on the source's box representation. [`expanded_box`](../../src/parkour_solver/scene/geometry.py), [`union_subtract/refine_support_cells`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/cells.py).

### 8.2 Keep overlapping safe regions as optional accelerators

Large overlapping convex regions can be useful local solve domains and shortcuts. But safe individual regions, a complete free-space union, preservation of path classes, and efficient route enumeration are four different properties. Seeded convex inflation does not guarantee the others, and our exact seed-preserving box cover is not the same algorithm as IRIS. [IRIS scope and coverage discussion][L9], [`free_transit_cover`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py).

Specifically, the current `free_transit_cover` starts with the raw free-space base, expands checked free cuboids, and discards boxes only when contained in another result. **Derivation:** containment removal does not itself remove covered base points, because every discarded point remains in a containing result; iterating this relation in the finite set ends in a retained maximal-by-containment box. “Not every possible maximal cuboid was enumerated” therefore does not imply missing free-position coverage. The unresolved guarantees concern route-class abstraction, bounded walk enumeration and dynamic realization, rather than a demonstrated hole in this geometric union. [`free_transit_cover`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`enumerate_semantic_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [coverage evidence](../../research/automatic-corridors/README.md).

**Proposal:** retain a covering/incidence substrate under any overlapping-region shortcut graph. Normalize shortcuts by their meaningful entering/leaving relation and contact/passage language, not by every particular chain of overlapping boxes. Unknown equivalence retains alternatives symbolically. If ordinary cell cuts create artificial order restrictions, allow paths through the underlying connected region language rather than impose one center polyline. [`Corridor.route_variants` and `semantic_events`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [`advance`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/progress.py), [current coverage counterexample and refinement rationale](../design/SPATIAL_CORRIDOR_REFACTOR_PLAN.md).

### 8.3 Generate interactions conditionally and before products

**Proposed order:** geometry suggests candidate support/passages; optimistic movement bounds order or rule out only universally excluded transitions; instantiated movement contexts query relevant obstacle phases; only active or unknown interactions create side/corner/contact alternatives; exact canonicalization happens before cross-obstacle products and numerical dispatch. This is an extension of existing shared relevance machinery, not a separate avoid-only relevance subsystem. [`annotate_normal_proposals`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`InteractionGate`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`ConstraintVariants`](../../src/parkour_solver/constraints/variants.py), [generation relevance §§1–3](../design/GENERATION_RELEVANCE_PLAN.md).

If the source family includes several incoming velocities, timings or mechanics, early exclusion must bound their union. “No ordinary jump found” is not “no route” when stepping/ceiling methods remain in scope. Conversely, a proven geometric separation or travel/apex violation across every admitted method is a legitimate early rejection. The present ordinary-proposal annotation explicitly treats missing normal profiles as unknown for other methods; retain that asymmetry. [`annotate_normal_proposals`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py), [`InteractionGate.query`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`minimum_distance/build_transition_graph`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/support_profiles.py).

### 8.4 Make refinement feedback informative without overstating it

**Proposal:** use successful low-level contact/passage traces to prioritize promising abstract connections, and use proved scoped contradictions to remove only the covered abstract refinements. Timeouts and local failures can influence service priority but cannot erase coverage. A coarse cyclic graph keeps untried options available while demand-driven refinement avoids an eager Cartesian product of every possible corner label. Layered exploration and lazy checking motivate the structure; proof scope and exact work ownership remain project obligations. [SyCLoP][L7], [Lazy PRM][L15], [CEGAR][L12]; [`route_queue.search_routes`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/corridor_queue.py), [`movement_frontier.persistent_programs`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/frontier.py).

## 11. Open design decisions and limits

These are unresolved **project questions**, not facts established by the cited literature. The referenced code/contracts explain why they must be answered before a coverage claim or implementation change.

- What exact contact/passage projection should count as a distinct progress family? Setup hops can share guidance while lower-level momentum histories remain distinct; a fixed purely geometric equivalence is insufficient. [§4](#4-route-identity-needs-both-contact-aware-passage-structure-and-constraint-semantics), [`motion_record`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py).
- Which early screens bound all incoming states/methods at an outer node, and which are valid only after particular decisions? Broad envelopes retain false positives; narrow ones need explicit provenance. [`InteractionGate`](../../src/parkour_solver/reachability/service.py), [`annotate_normal_proposals`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/regions.py).
- Which portal/cell contractions preserve all meaningful entry/exit and contact alternatives? A connectivity-preserving graph need not enumerate every desired route class. [L2][L2], [`route_variants`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/corridors.py), [`advance`](../../src/parkour_solver/spatial/compat/progress.py).
- How should a broad shared guide be refined when actual decisions choose a different contact/passage? Guidance reuse, physical acceptance and exact model dedup need separate identities. [`solve_identity`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/identity.py), [`SemanticCorridorAdapter`](../../src/parkour_solver/search/compat/semantic_adapter.py).
- How much time is spent in guard expansion versus compilation, physical avoidance and reporting? The available wrapper-time subtraction does not answer this. [Audit](../../research/automatic-corridors/routing-review-2026-11-03/README.md).
- Exact future step/grind probe geometry, intentional wall hits and other currently excluded mechanics require their own model/coverage work; this research does not silently add them. Complete arbitrary-map geometry also needs the actual obstacle data, including B6's unspecified extension. [Current scope and known limits](../CORRIDOR_IMPLEMENTATION_RESUME.md).
