# Stratfinder levers: problem formulation, pain ranking, and approach research (2026-08-07)

Follow-up to the template spike recorded in `difficulty-metric-handoff.md` (seventh session). Question: where does the template generator hurt most, and what are the best-evidenced ways to fix it? Sources: the 250 ms benchmark artifacts (`core/build/hpk-metric/template-*-250ms.*`), a constraint-drop audit of the suspect wins, two follow-up benchmark runs (tail retry at 2000 ms, d1-d6 telemetry at 250 ms), a reachability pre-screen prototype, and a 2026-08-07 literature sweep (Q1 mode graphs, Q2 screening ladders, Q3 derivative-free constrained search).

## 1. The problem, stated mathematically

Per tick t the state is s_t = (x_t, z_t, vx_t, vz_t). Two controls per tick: a discrete input vector k_t (key set W/A/S/D, JUMP, SPRINT, amplifiers) and a continuous facing theta_t. The collision-free X/Z dynamics (ExactJumpModel, byte-exact):

- v' = v_t, zeroed if |v|^2 < 9e-6 (inertia gate; per-axis 0.005/0.003 in legacy eras)
- jump impulse on fire ticks (grounded and JUMP): +0.2 * (-sin theta, cos theta) when sprinting
- input acceleration: a_t(k_t) * R(theta_t) * m(k_t), where a_t is a scalar magnitude fixed by ground/air, slip, sprint, amplifier, and m is the normalized key direction
- move: pos_{t+1} = pos_t + v'
- friction: v_{t+1} = v' * f_t, with f_t = slip * 0.91 grounded, 0.91 airborne

with all trig through the 65536-bucket integer sine LUT in float32. The grounded schedule is authored by jump ticks and arc lengths; collisions are never simulated, world geometry enters only as per-tick constraint sets C_t (boxes, planes).

The full stratfinder problem: over (k_0..k_T, theta_0..theta_T, start position in a free-start domain), minimize the difficulty metric subject to s_t in C_t for all constrained ticks, T roughly 20 to 50. Structural facts that everything below leans on: position is linear in the accumulated (sin, cos) contributions; the only nonconvexities are the per-tick unit circle, the rare inertia-gate indicator, and the discrete k-sequence choice.

The template grammar is a restriction of the k-sequence space to the human strat vocabulary: run(d)+jam, pessi(k), fmm(k), Mark(side,k), bwmm prefix arcs composed with a follow-up, each realized as a /nt variant (dF=0 chain, stays in the closed-form solve path) and a free-yaw variant (CMA-ES path). The benchmark enumerates roughly 70 instances per jump and cold-solves each independently. That enumeration-of-independent-solves architecture, not the vocabulary itself, is what most of the findings below push against.

## 2. What hurts most, ranked

P1. The multi-arc redirect tail. Eight captures get no feasible template at 250 ms (j081, j121, j140, j335, j345, j1150, j716, j154). These are exactly the jumps whose human lines are TAS-tight (window minima of 1 to 2 sine buckets), so a cold 250 ms CMA-ES start on a 30-plus-tick problem with a sliver-width feasible set is the wall. The tail retry (section 5) shows the 250 ms tail was substantially budget-limited: three of the eight crack at 2000 ms, leaving a five-jump d10-d11 hard core.

P2. Realization correctness. Constraints at ticks at or before the human fire tick are dropped wholesale, and F/DF constraints are dropped everywhere. Audit results: j135's -5.47 "win" is fake (the four 0.125-wide wall-hug boxes plus the DF chain on ticks 1-4, the entire waza, are dropped; the runway rectangle reimposes only a plus-minus 0.3 version). j030's numbers are meaningless (its defining 5.4375 landing line sits on tick 37 with landingTick 36, so it is dropped everywhere; needs the known re-save). j053's -1.09 survives audit: what was dropped there is repositioning discipline that free-start legitimately subsumes, and the head-hitter ceiling is enforced structurally by the preserved hop schedule. Systemic fix: tick-specific pre-fire range boxes cannot transfer to a different approach, but pre-fire one-sided planes (walls) are geometry, not timing; clamp them onto every generated approach tick instead of dropping them. That one change removes the fake-win class.

P3. Throughput. 70 instances times 250 ms with most instances infeasible and burning the full budget. The measured fix stack is in section 4.

P4. No in-game gate. Nothing template-generated is baked, realized, or V-gated yet; per the standing rule, no template strat is real until it replays through SimulatorEntity.

## 3. Verdicts on the four floated ideas

- CMA-ES with collision wired in: no, as an inner loop. The model boundary (X/Z plus tick, collisions never simulated) is what makes the inner loop microsecond-cheap; the entity is the only collision-true oracle and is orders of magnitude too slow, and constraints are already the collision abstraction. If collision-awareness in-search is ever needed, the cheap form is constraint generation: post-check with SweptCollision, add the violated wall plane as a new constraint, re-solve. Not simulation inside CMA-ES.
- Accept only dual-solvable (closed-form) jumps: right instinct, wrong form. As an acceptance filter it biases the vocabulary toward no-turn strats. As an ordering it is the core of the screening ladder: /nt instances first at millisecond cost, free-yaw instances only where the /nt sweep leaves gaps. Section 5 quantifies the actual /nt vs free cost split.
- Does the problem decompose into something existing: yes. The template chain is a path through a graph whose vertices are (strat, parameter) modes with a convex (disk-relaxed) program inside each vertex and timing-handoff constraints on edges. That is Graphs of Convex Sets, verbatim. See section 4, Q1.
- Human freezes some inputs: the templates already are frozen input schedules, and the /nt variant is frozen yaw shape. Further manual freezing adds friction for marginal search-space reduction; agreed not worth it.

## 4. Literature sweep (2026-08-07, verified citations)

Q1, joint discrete plus continuous structure. Graphs of Convex Sets (Marcucci et al., SIAM J. Opt. 2024; Science Robotics 2023) solves shortest paths through graphs of convex programs as one MICP with an empirically tight relaxation; Drake ships a maintained implementation (GcsTrajectoryOptimization). Extensions cover our variants: GCS* (Chia et al., RSS 2024) for implicit forward search, shortest walks (Morozov et al., arXiv 2507.10878, 2025) for hybrid mode sequences with sub-second online solves on graphs of 16-22 vertices, SDP-tightened nonconvex vertices (Graesdal et al., RSS 2024), and the decade-old trig-in-MIP precedent (Deits-Tedrake footstep MIQP, Humanoids 2014). Fit: adaptation-grade, not drop-in; the per-mode disk-relaxed program already exists in-repo (the settled MIQCP reformulation), GCS is the way to share one relaxation across the template choice instead of 70 cold solves. Byte-exactness still comes from snap plus ExactJumpModel verify; the GCS layer is a proposer only.

Q2, screening many mostly-infeasible candidates. Best-evidenced architecture is successive halving (Jamieson-Talwalkar 2016; Hyperband, Li et al. JMLR 2018; ASHA, MLSys 2020): rungs of increasing per-candidate budget, keep the top 1/eta per rung, eta 3 to 4, roughly equal total budget per rung. Two load-bearing add-ons: rung 0 must be a sound necessary-condition filter, not a heuristic (an unsound cheap rung can eliminate the only feasible template); the MINLP name for it is FBBT, interval propagation over the recurrence, which our linear-in-(sin,cos) dynamics make trivial. And irace-style adaptive capping (Perez Caceres et al., LION 2017): once any template is feasible, cap later solves at a multiple of the incumbent's time-to-feasibility instead of the fixed full budget. The feasibility pump is the wrong tool (heuristic finder, not sound rejector); constrained BO (SCBO, FuRBO) targets expensive evaluations and does not fit a microsecond model.

Q3, beating multistart CMA-ES at n from 10 to 50. Stated plainly: nothing reliably dominates tuned restarted CMA-ES on constrained multimodal suites at this dimensionality. Two evidenced partial wins: NOMAD 4 / MADS with progressive barrier outperforms CMA-ES at small evaluation budgets on bbob-constrained up to d 40 (Audet et al., GECCO Companion 2022), which matches the per-restart regime of our screen; and the CEC constrained lineage (LSHADE44, epsilonMAg-ES) whose active ingredient is epsilon-level lexicographic feasibility ordering plus cheap repair, not the search distribution (Hellwig-Beyer, arXiv 2405.05005), a comparator swap that composes with the existing multistart. AL-CMA-ES variants and feasibility-first BO are explicitly not recommended for this cost profile. No published "bandit over restart seeds" winner exists.

## 5. Experiments run this session

### 5a. Diagnosis runs (pre-fix realization)

Tail retry, 2000 ms budget over d7/9/10/11/12 (`template-report-tail2000ms.txt`). Three former NONE captures crack: j081 (d7) 9/70 feasible, best run5+jam at -0.114 below human; j121 (d9) 5/70, best run0+jam at -2.043 (caution: j121's human baseline is one of the TAS-tight reconstructions, so part of that delta may be baseline inflation; the feasibility itself is real); j154 (d12) 7/70 feasible, no baseline (defective save). Coverage explodes on the barely-feasible captures: j925 goes 1/70 to 27/70 (best fmm4 at -1.826, versus +1.195 at 250 ms), j082 goes 1/70 to 34/70, j188 3/70 to 13/70, j108 1/70 to 9/70. Still NONE at 2000 ms: j140, j335, j345 (d10), j1150, j716 (d11). Conclusion: the 250 ms NONE tail was mostly budget, and the residual is precisely the nix and winged-neo cluster where the joint GCS lever (section 4, Q1) aims. j135's fake win is unchanged at 2000 ms, consistent with the P2 diagnosis.

Per-instance telemetry, 250 ms over d1-d6 (`template-instances-timing250ms.csv`, 1260 instances). 1007 of 1260 instances fail, and every failure burns the full budget (min 249, median 251 ms): roughly 80 percent of all wall time is spent on infeasible instances. Feasible instances are cheap: /nt solves have median 28 ms (min 23), free-yaw median 101 ms, and only 9 of 253 feasible solves ever reached CMA-ES; closed form, receding horizon, SLP, and relaxation-recovery chains carry the rest. Implication: the throughput lever is not a faster success path, it is failing fast, plus /nt-first ordering.

ReachBound prototype (`metriclab/ReachBound.java`, `HpkReachScreen`, PKC_REACH=1, `reach-report.txt`). A sound per-tick speed bound derived from the exact step terms (contact friction, accel magnitude, sprint-jump impulse), checked pairwise against the constraint gates parsed from the instance save. Screening all 2380 instances takes about 70 microseconds each with zero soundness violations against every solver-feasible instance on record, and prunes 526 of 1807 known-failed instances (29 percent). The rate is bimodal: approach-length-limited captures prune 50 to 52 of 70 (j001, j014, j018, j135), while overshoot and corridor captures prune 0, because a speed-norm bound cannot see direction. The v2 with real headroom is per-axis directional interval propagation over the linear-in-(sin,cos) recurrence, the actual FBBT of section 4 Q2. Note: d8 captures (j101, j105, j123) had no solver telemetry in the cross-check (neither follow-up run covered d8); their prune counts are 0 so there is no soundness exposure.

### 5b. Lever implementation and validation (2026-08-07, second pass)

Realization fix, implemented and validated (`template-report-postfix250ms.txt`). `StratTemplates.realize` now clips the runway rectangle by the envelope of pre-fire range constraints (padded 0.3) and pre-fire one-sided planes, and clamps those planes onto every generated approach tick. Effects at 250 ms: j135 goes to 0/70 (the fake -5.47 class is gone entirely); the fix also exposed two wins the audit had wrongly passed, j001 (+0.32 post-fix, was -1.10) and j053 (+2.22 post-fix, was -1.09), both of which were riding the loose runway rectangle; the section 2 audit verdict on j053 is hereby corrected. The exact-tie reproductions all survive: j158 pessi4/nt -0.003, j032 pessi1/nt -0.001, j066 pessi1 -0.001, and j276 becomes an effective tie at -0.004. Honest at-or-below-human count at 250 ms: 10 of 19 baselined captures in the d1-d6+d9 set.

Screening ladder, implemented and measured (`HpkLadderScreen`, PKC_LADDER=1; `ladder-report.txt`, `ladder-report-pruneonly.txt`; d1-d6, pre-fix population so recall joins are clean). Prune-only (ReachBound rung 0, flat 250 ms): 180 s wall versus 278 s flat, a 35 percent saving at 97 percent recall, with the 3 misses attributable to solver run-to-run variance (borderline instances flip between runs; the same variance also produced 5 new finds). Aggressive SHA (60 ms rung, promote top third by met/total, 250 ms top): 109 s wall, a 2.5x saving, but recall drops to 64 percent and the losses concentrate in the pessi/backwalled family (j158 missed 20 of 22, j068 found 0): the met/total ranking signal anti-correlates there, because a 60 ms failed solve's constraint-met count says little about closeness to feasibility. Verdict: prune-only ships now; SHA promotion needs a violation-magnitude ranking signal before it is trustworthy.

Hard-core assault, widened vocabulary (`PKC_TEMPLATE_WIDE=1`: run to 12, fmm to 8, pessi to 11, mark to 6, four extra bwmm follow-ups; 52 plans, 104 instances) at 4000 ms over d10-d11, post-fix (`ladder-report-assault.txt`). Four of the five remaining NONE jumps fall: j140 11 feasible (including wide-only run9/run10), j345 True Nix Neo 1 feasible (fmm5), j1150 Nix Neo 28 feasible (including wide-only pessi9-11, markA5/A6, bwmm+fmm3/4, bwmm+pessi2/3), j716 13 feasible (run family plus the whole bwmm family). j925 as control rises to 40 feasible. Wide-only labels appearing in the winning lists directly validate the vocabulary widening. Only j335 stays NONE.

2000 ms rerun of the 250 ms NONE set under the user start rects (`template-report-rerun2000.txt`, per-capture JVMs). j158's exact tie is budget-flaky no more: pessi4/nt at -0.003 with 29/105 feasible; baked and deployed (sixth save). Eleven tail captures flip feasible but every at-or-below-human line among them is free-yaw (j121 -2.04, j123 -0.62, j108 -0.21, j081 -0.06), i.e. outside the playability rule, so nothing else baked. Still NONE at 2000 ms: j001, j014, j276, j028, j055, j053, j135, j335, j716. Conclusion: within the playable variant classes the hand-coded grammar is exhausted at 6 covered captures; the binding constraint is vocabulary, and the planned mining of the user's ~4000-strat server corpus is aimed exactly at the missing approach idioms.

Disk-relaxation infeasibility certificates (`HpkRelaxExportScreen` PKC_RELAX_EXPORT=1 exporting per-instance schedule and gates; `relax_solve.py`, scipy HiGHS LP: accel, sprint-jump impulse, and a 0.0072 inertia-cancel slack merged into one per-tick disturbance disk, 24-gon outer approximation, gates padded 1e-3; all relaxation supersets, so INFEASIBLE is a sound certificate). Over d9-d11 wide (936 instances): j335 has 90 of 104 relaxation-feasible instances (only the long fmm7-8 and pessi7-11 arcs are certified dead), so the last holdout is a search problem, not a vocabulary problem; the solver, not the grammar, is what fails there. j135 has 68 of 104 certified infeasible (every fmm, pessi, and mark timing), proving the waza gap is a genuine vocabulary limit. Zero conflicts between certificates and any solver-feasible outcome. The certificates also position the relaxation as a future ladder rung between ReachBound and the first real solve.
