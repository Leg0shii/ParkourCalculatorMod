## Per-hypothesis evidence

### H1: chain FBBT does not fix gates before branching. DEAD.

The #422 claim (DESIGN.md step 1, "the single biggest lever": FBBT fixes almost every gate, only k <= ~6 straddling ticks remain) is measured false at the root on every single capture.

- `research/step1/h1_fbbt.py`, per-axis forward interval propagation over the disk relaxation |u_t| <= mMag(t), all 30 real corpus structs: ambiguous gate count k = kx + kz ~ 2(n-1) everywhere. j1150 k=90, j716 82, j021 76, j345 96, j153 166. Zero captures below k = n.
- j003 (n=176, the design's own flagged worst case; exported this session, modern combined-XZ gate, eps 0.003): root-ambiguous on 175 of 176 ticks.
- Structural cause, not an artifact: the relaxation's reachable-velocity set is a disk of radius ~ mMag/(1-f) ~ 1.1 b/t containing the origin with huge margin, so |v| < eps ~ 0.005 is always reachable; forcing |v| >= eps is a nonconvex magnitude lower bound that interval propagation cannot express. The backward pass bounds weighted sums of many inputs to landing-box tolerances (0.01..1 b) and cannot pin a single 0.005 carry. OBBT inherits the same obstruction (and is already on the DESIGN.md trap list).
- Gate determinism is arc-depth-coupled: `h1b_arcwidth.py` (arcs bracketed to a reference +-delta) shows k collapsing only near delta <= 1 deg, and even there a low-velocity tail keeps k at 48 (j155), 19 (j021), 12 (j757), 9 (loopmm). The "branch arcs ~8 levels, then branch k<=6 gates" cost model is wrong; the tree interleaves arc and gate branching on the tail.
- The recorded byte-exact solutions ARE nearly gate-deterministic (j1150 fires 10 gate events total); FBBT just cannot see it. The mechanism that actually resolves gates, measured working in the PoC, is: fix schedule, solve the continuous disk relaxation, replay through ExactJumpModel, read off the gate pattern, fold it (pattern-aware JumpLinearModel + velocityWalls), iterate to a fixed point (2 rounds on j1150, fidelity 1.3e-2 -> <= 1.5e-4). The robotics literature names this Staged Contact Optimization and documents its failure mode (premature mode commitment), which fixes the guard: a replay that violates a folded gate reopens that gate into the 3-way branch.

Consequence: FBBT is demoted to arc/bound tightening inside nodes. Gate handling = folding first, exact 3-way branch as the certificate closer on the residual tail.

### H2: the bucket window is real but only inside a basin. SIMPLIFIES (conditional).

- Direct corpus measurement (`research/step1/h2_bucketdist.py`, sphere QCQP at FeasTol 1e-9, gaps <= 9.4e-5): per-tick bucket distance W between the continuous optimum facing and the recorded byte-exact facing is HUGE cross-family: W_median 11..621 buckets, W_p90 1298..6002, W_max up to 12560 (j021, j144, j155, j716, j718, j757, j345). The continuous global optimum is routinely a different strategy family than the recorded solution (objective deltas 1e-4..1.8e-2 with compensating schedules downstream). The global claim "byte optimum within 1-2 buckets of the continuous optimum" is falsified as stated.
- The basin-local form survives and is strong: on loopmm the sphere optimum reproduces the recorded facings to W = 0 buckets on >= 90% of ticks (median and p90 both 0.0; the one large-W tick is t=0, the single modulus-throttled tick). Session-2 knife-edge measurements stand: within the pinned family, byte optima sit 1-2 buckets from the continuous optimum, and one bucket (0.00549 deg) moves the trajectory ~1e-4 b.
- Leaf-window sizing is chain-generation dependent (H3, check 2): the legacy 1.8/1.12 float sine chain puts the realized (sin, cos) pair up to 4.8e-5 off the unit circle, so the true grid argmax can sit up to ~50 buckets from the costate direction; nearest-bucket snapping can silently lose ~5e-4 b per adversarial tick. On 26.x the pair-norm noise is 4.0e-8 and the window is 1-3 buckets.

Consequence: the angle side becomes a small enumeration/MILP layer ONLY after the outer layer fixes family and gate pattern. Any code path that applies the 2W+1 reduction before structure commitment reintroduces the falsified global claim. Leaf = bucket-window scan (not nearest-bucket snap), then pinned-bucket walk + ulp nudge for knife-edge closure; RENS-style exact rounding over simultaneously ambiguous ticks is the formalized version.

### H3: the costate is a warm start and a bound, never a feasibility engine. SIMPLIFIES with hard limits.

- The pointwise Hamiltonian argmax is real: dualizing only walls, the Lagrangian is separable per tick; u*_t = m_t g_t/||g_t|| with costate g_t = c_t - sum_j lam_j A_jt (CostateDualSolver.java:397-424). The backward adjoint recursion equals the code's coefficient chain to 6.1e-12 (verified on 5 real exports). Active intermediate walls do NOT break the argmax form (they superpose truncated chains); ticks after the last active wall are pinned to the objective direction outright. Exact conditions: fixed gate pattern, dual-optimal lam with every g_t != 0 (equivalently the disk relaxation saturates every modulus).
- The DESIGN.md step 2 claim "strong feasible incumbent from tick 0" is unsound: measured on 7 real captures, the lam=0 Hamiltonian-max controls replay dynamics-feasible but violate walls by 1.51 (j005), 2.13 (j019), 1.45 (loopmm), 4.14 (f2f), 0.08 (j155), 12.29 (j1150), 8.63 (j716) blocks; hopeless against e-5 corridors. What actually filled the zero-incumbents-cold gap in the PoC was wall homotopy + MIP starts + pattern folding.
- The CostateDualSolver plateau (pgres ~2.4 on thousand/1-dup2) is structural, two layers. (1) pgres is definitionally a recovery meter (pgres <= tol iff the full-modulus pointwise recovery is tol-feasible and complementary); on coupled-corridor instances the dual minimum sits at a kink where some g_t = 0, so NO lam yields a feasible pointwise recovery and pgres has a positive floor. Reproduced in pure python on loopmm: minViol frozen at 0.7901 from 10k through 300k iterations while the dual VALUE is tight to 1.03e-3. Single-jump and clean multi-jump controls (j005, f2f) converge to ~0 in the same harness; corridor/coupling degeneracy is the trigger, not multi-jump per se. (2) EPS2=1e-14 smoothing makes the kink a ~1e7-condition cliff, and even exact convergence would recover an INTERIOR u whose direction is 1e-7-norm noise. No iteration count fixes this; the two legal consumptions of an unconverged dual are its VALUE as a weak-duality prune bound (valid at any lam) and its costates as seeds. Hard rule: no node logic may gate on pgres.
- Degenerate ticks are where the argmax is silent: near-zero node costate or interior relaxed modulus (the chatter set). Those must be decided by arc branching; that is the honest limit of "boundary conditions only".
- The thousand/1-dup2 confirmation run is blocked: the capture is not on this machine. The loopmm python reproduction plus the disk throttled-tick data stand in.

Consequence: keep the costate machinery in three demoted roles: O(n * activeWalls) dive at deep nodes (arc-clipped, bucket-window-snapped, replayed), tail pinning after the last active wall, dual value as an anytime prune bound.

### H4: decompose by state chaining, never by an enumerated velocity window.

- Interface sensitivity (`research/step1/h4_sensitivity.py`): a velocity carried across a ground-touch split is amplified into the terminal objective by C_s = f(1-f^(n-s))/(1-f), measured 6.85..10.05 on j1150/j155 splits (saturation 10.11). Holding the 1e-4 corridor then needs ~1e-5 b/axis interface resolution; a +-0.35 b/t window at that step is ~4.9e9 cells. Dead.
- The reachable interface set is sparse and discrete, not a continuum: existing byte-exact j1150 replays show two feasible families 3.9e-2 apart in interface velocity at t=12, re-converging to 5.5e-4 by t=37, and bit-identical interface velocities within a family across a 5e-5 objective spread.
- The objective is not a function of the interface velocity alone: the p2/p3 pair differ 8.65e-3 in interface velocity but 1.5e-5 in objective (the downstream schedule compensates). A velocity-keyed segment oracle mis-ranks strategies; the exact state (or a costate) must cross the interface.
- The working form is already built: LongRunSolver solves a sliding ~10-jump window to optimality and chains the exact byte-exact exit state, so the concatenation is feasible by construction; cross-tick coupling decays as f^g (0.293 at g=13, 0.095 at g=25, 0.023 at g=40), licensing the horizon (Shin-Zavala decay).
- Disk throttled-tick geography (existing h1h2 data + this session): interior-modulus ticks cluster in run-up/redirect/backwalk phases and DO sit on several ground-touch splits (j008b t14, j345 t24, j155 t10-11), where windowing is fragile. j153 is interior on its whole first half (49/84), j003 on all 176/176 ticks.
- Stratfinder comparison: its zSolver is exactly the velocity-window-target oracle, and its README concedes the solutions are "not really constructive". That is the honest ceiling of the interface: a coarse feasibility filter and warm-start source, which is what VelocityFinder fields remain.
- Unrun (deferred to step 2): the certificate-level check that a windowed COPT solve equals the joint optimum on a 3-jump capture, plus the reachable-interface bucket count. Needs a StructureDump split-export mode (PKC_STRUCT_SPLIT_TICK).

### H7: no single frozen translation layer; the anchor-local contract is the layer. DEAD (strong form).

- Clamp-free single layer: worst byte-vs-linear wall error ~1e-2 on gate-active jumps (j144 3.75e-2, j716 1.28e-2, j021 5.15e-3, j153 5.92e-3 at n=84), 100-1000x the e-5 corridors. Only gate-inactive loose jumps fit (j828 7.05e-6).
- Folded layer: residual <= 1.5e-4 (j1150), still LARGER than the tightest measured corridor (j154: 9e-5 budget, four walls ridden at e-6, tightest proven-safe collision clearance 4e-7). A sound a-priori interval envelope is necessarily >= the empirical residual, so a rigorous never-recheck contract can never certify knife-edge jumps. Uniform guessed margins (3e-5) measurably cut off the true manifold; constant offsets fail across combo flips (predicted 0.010, replayed 0.034; parametric-MILP value-function theory explains why: wall margins are RHS continuation, schedule changes are structural jumps, recompute per schedule, never transport).
- The working contract (measured, j154 closed to maxViol 0.0 twice): bucket-exact direction constants (exact) + per-wall SIGNED SLP margins measured at the current incumbent's byte-exact replay, re-anchored each round + collision clearance >= 2e-6 baked into the SOLVE + ExactJumpModel replay as the sole certificate. Rigorous radii (Gappa/affine style, float64 ~1e-11 scale) remain an optional search-side pruning upgrade on loose jumps only.

## The reduction stack (what ARCH-2 actually is, ranked)

1. **Scope split first: angle solving and stratfinding are separate problems.** The inner ARCH-2 solver takes FIXED inputs and finds facings; nothing in it searches keys, ever. The grammar / easy-class results (H5, H6, H8) apply ONLY to the outer no-turn stratfinder layer, where the input schedule IS the search object: there, proposals come from 10-family templates (1-4 segments, ranked by the library.json prior), compiled to the no-turn MIQCP shape (one shared facing circle + per-tick combo integers + sprint latch + edge caps + /4 relabel quotient), escalating to free per-tick facing only on certified infeasibility. The j003 finding routes differently because j003 is an inner (fixed-input) instance: its disk optimum is interior on all 176 ticks, so with fixed keys the byte-exact realization of interior thrust is facing chatter, the costate is silent everywhere, and those arcs are exactly what the B&B must branch (item 3). No input search is implied or possible there.
2. **Gate patterns are fixed by incumbent-driven folding, not propagation.** Relax-solve -> replay -> extract pattern -> fold (pattern-aware JumpLinearModel + velocityWalls) -> iterate to the fixed point. A replay that violates a folded gate reopens it into the exact 3-way branch. Certification budget for gate branching = the low-velocity tail after folding, NOT k <= 6 at the root.
3. **ARCH-2 must branch on: angle arcs, and residual gate binaries.** Arc bisection with the chord cut (plus cheap endpoint tangent cuts) on the ticks the node relaxation itself flags (throttled/interior modulus, near-zero costate); 3-way gate split on replay-falsified or tail gates. Branch ordering comes free from the disk solve's throttled-tick geography; no pseudocost machinery.
4. **The continuous kernel stays DiskSocpKernel + arc chord cut, IPM at nodes.** Vindicated at every scale (disk-vs-sphere 2.1e-7..1.6e-3 corpus-wide, 1.7e-4 at n=84; near rank-1 SDP; j003 n=176 continuous solves in 0.33 s). The k-gon dual-simplex build is demoted to measured-need.
5. **Incumbents come from wall homotopy + MIP starts + folding.** The homotopy ladder (relax walls 0.05 -> tighten staged -> fold candidate's own pattern -> SLP walk) is the only measured cold-discovery mechanism on knife edges and equals Scholtes continuation with known discipline (small rungs near active-set flips, restart on path loss). MIP starts come from witnesses, previous rounds, or switching-cost-aware rounding of the disk trajectory. The costate contributes a node dive at depth, tail pinning, and an anytime dual bound; it is not the incumbent engine.
6. **The translation layer is anchor-local.** Per-wall signed SLP margins at the current byte-exact anchor, re-anchored every round; bucket-exact direction constants; collision clearance >= 2e-6 in the solve; ExactJumpModel replay is the ONLY certificate (gap=0 on the finite grid). Never transport margins across schedules.
7. **Leaf = bucket-window scan, then pinned walk + ulp nudge.** Window 1-3 buckets on 26.x, up to +-50 on legacy (float sine-table pair-norm noise 4.8e-5); RENS-style exact rounding over the simultaneously ambiguous ticks, capped to active-wall neighborhoods. Basin-local only, after structure commitment.
8. **Long runs = LongRunSolver state-chained windows.** Already built; VelocityFinder fields demote to a coarse feasibility filter and warm-start oracle. No enumerated velocity-window interface anywhere.
9. **The one new build: the structure-pool driver.** One-tree no-good-cut enumeration over (schedule, pattern) at moderate relaxation, homotopy-tighten each survivor with per-round pattern re-extraction, keep byte-exact survivors; proximity search replaces the starving min-edges objective; pool-dry proves nothing, the certificate stays gap=0 replay. H5 makes its proposal space dozens of templates; H8 quotients it by 1e13..1e38.

## Literature adoptions (named identifications)

- Inertia gate = state-triggered dead-zone / vanishing constraint (MPVC, FESD); explains why CIA/rounding cannot touch it and why the exact 3-way branch is right. CIA rounding-gap theory (Sager-Bock-Diehl; Kirches-Lenders-Manns 2020) is the citation for why no rounding regime replaces exact branching once coupling walls exist.
- Gate-pattern folding fixed point = Staged Contact Optimization (fix mode from relaxed solve, then polish), with its premature-commitment guard: falsified folds reopen to branching.
- Wall homotopy = Scholtes relax-then-tighten / graduated optimization; parametric-MILP value-function theory (Blair-Jeroslow) licenses continuation along wall RHS and forbids margin transport across schedules.
- Structure-pool driver = one-tree no-good-cut near-optimal enumeration (Danna et al.) + proximity search for the integer edge objective (Fischetti-Monaci) + switching-cost-aware CIA rounding for MIP starts (Bestehorn-Hansknecht-Kirches-Manns) + dwell-time/TV encodings for the latch and edge caps (Zeile-Robuschi-Sager).
- Leaf discipline = RENS optimal rounding (Berthold) over the bucket window; arc relaxation = QC chord + endpoint tangent envelope (Coffrin-Hijazi-Van Hentenryck), both as plain Wall rows in DiskSocpKernel.
- Depth-level gate resolution = LCP 3-case sign-forcing pivots (Stewart-Trinkle/Lemke): when a propagated carry interval at depth is single-signed and clear of the eps band, resolve without branching.
- Certified-FP field: exact-replay-as-terminal-certificate (Rump/Neumaier split) confirms the existing architecture; SMT-FP, Taylor models, and smoothing are rejected for this problem; Krawczyk existence boxes are a Phase-0+ experiment for the homotopy-to-grid seam on loose/medium jumps.
- Rejected wholesale with measurements: FBBT/OBBT root gate-fixing, perspective gate hulls (linear gated term), feasibility pump/jump (corridor failures are not integrality failures), enumerated velocity windows, alphaBB/smooth trig underestimators (the deployed nonlinearity is a step function), first-order dual/ADMM as certifier (C3-class stall = our measured plateau).
